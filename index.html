The case (real, 2017 to 2018)

Your role: a control-room analyst in July 2018. A WhatsApp video is being forwarded in your district. It looks like CCTV: two men on a motorbike take a small boy. The caption says “Child lifters in your area! Forward to save children.” People are frightened and crowds are gathering at village entrances. The same clip is being shared in several states.

Your job: use the slide 24 flow to find out what the video really is, then decide what to tell the public. All questions can be answered from the public pages linked below and from free tools. You never search for a private person.

What really happened

The video was made in 2016 in Karachi by an ad agency for an NGO, as a reconstruction to warn parents. In India it was cropped so the ending vanished, and it spread with false captions alongside other old videos. Fact-checkers had exposed it in 2017, yet in July 2018 five men were killed in Dhule district, Maharashtra, after such videos created panic. The case shows why a quick status check, finding the original and a calm, sourced advisory matter.
